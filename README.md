# JobHustle

### Find the right professional. Build the right crew. Get the job done.

JobHustle is a local service hiring platform that connects customers with skilled professionals such as electricians, plumbers, cleaners, gardeners, painters, movers, handymen, and other service providers.

The platform makes it easier for customers to find trusted professionals, compare services and prices, create bookings, and track the complete service process.

JobHustle also allows professionals to work individually or form service packages and teams for jobs that require multiple skills.

---

## Problem Statement

Finding reliable local workers is still difficult for many people.

Customers usually depend on:

- Friends and family recommendations
- WhatsApp groups
- Local contacts
- Random online searches

Even after finding someone, customers may not know:

- What services the worker provides
- Their experience
- Their price
- Their availability
- Whether they are reliable
- Whether they can handle a larger job

At the same time, many skilled local workers depend mainly on word-of-mouth to find customers.

They may have good skills and experience but do not have a proper digital platform to:

- Show their services
- Set their prices
- Receive bookings
- Manage customers
- Build a professional profile
- Work together with other professionals

Another major problem is that many real-life jobs require more than one service.

For example, when moving into a new house, a customer may need:

- Cleaning
- Electrical work
- Plumbing
- Furniture assembly
- Painting

Finding and coordinating different workers separately can be difficult and time-consuming.

---

## Our Solution

JobHustle provides one platform where customers can discover, compare, and hire local professionals.

Customers can search for professionals based on their service, location, pricing, experience, and availability.

Instead of supporting only individual workers, CrewNest introduces two special types of service packages.

### Multitasking Package

A single professional can offer multiple related services as one package.

For example:

**Complete Move-In Care**

- Home cleaning
- Furniture assembly
- Painting touch-ups

The customer can book one professional who is capable of handling multiple tasks.

### Team Package

Multiple professionals can work together as one service team.

For example:

**Full Home Makeover**

- Cleaner
- Electrician
- Plumber

One professional acts as the team lead while the other professionals work as team members.

The customer sends only one booking request instead of contacting every worker separately.

## Main Features

### For Customers

- Create and manage a customer account
- Browse service categories
- Search for professionals
- Filter professionals based on services and location
- View professional profiles
- View experience and service prices
- Book individual professionals
- Book multitasking packages
- Book team packages
- View booking details
- Track booking status
- Contact the booked professional
- Confirm service completion
- Give ratings and reviews

---

### For Service Providers

- Create a professional profile
- Add profession and experience
- Choose service location
- Add services
- Set hourly, daily, or monthly rates
- Receive customer booking requests
- Accept or reject requests
- View active bookings
- Request service completion
- Create multitasking packages
- Create team packages
- Work as a team member or team lead
- View customer reviews

---

## Booking Workflow

The basic booking process is:

```text
Customer selects a professional
            ↓
Customer creates a booking
            ↓
Booking status: Pending
            ↓
Provider receives the request
            ↓
Provider accepts the booking
            ↓
Booking status: Accepted
            ↓
Provider completes the work
            ↓
Provider requests completion
            ↓
Customer confirms completion
            ↓
Booking status: Completed
            ↓
Customer can leave a review
